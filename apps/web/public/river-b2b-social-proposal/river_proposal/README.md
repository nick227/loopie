# River — B2B Social Media Pivot

This proposal pack defines **River**, a new first-class B2B social surface inside LOOPIE.

River extends the existing business-profile, advertisement, messaging, CRM, embed, and engagement systems into a native social channel that businesses can use immediately without connecting an external social account.

## Core idea

A business should be able to:

1. Maintain a public LOOPIE business profile.
2. Publish organic posts and existing advertisements to **River**.
3. Distribute the same content to authenticated external social platforms where supported.
4. Track impressions, engagement, clicks, replies, conversions, and downstream CRM activity through one normalized engagement model.
5. Use River as a zero-friction proving ground for social publishing, analytics, campaign experiments, and engagement tracking.

## Product thesis

River should not initially compete with broad consumer social networks. Its job is narrower and more valuable:

> Give businesses a native place to publish, discover one another, demonstrate work, market offers, and generate measurable B2B engagement — while LOOPIE learns how to publish and track social activity across every channel.

River is therefore both a customer-facing network and the reference implementation for LOOPIE's broader social publishing platform.
