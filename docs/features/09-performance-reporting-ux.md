# Performance & Reporting UX

## High-value questions
1. What is running?
2. What have we spent?
3. What results did we get?
4. Which creative works best?
5. Which platform works best?

## Primary funnel
**Views → Clicks → Leads → Sales**

Example:
Raw Customer Stories  
$1,240 spent | $8,400 sales  
42,100 views → 812 clicks → 24 leads → 7 sales

## Creative comparison
Video A  
18.4k views → 390 clicks → 14 leads → 5 sales

Video B  
15.2k views → 301 clicks → 7 leads → 2 sales

## Platform comparison
Minimum:
- spend
- leads
- sales
- CPL
- revenue/ROAS when available

## Creative × platform
The system must answer:
- how Creative A performed on Meta
- how Creative A performed on TikTok
- how Meta performed across all creatives

## Dashboard
Top line:
- Spend
- Leads
- CPL
- Active Campaigns

## Principle
Avoid a giant analytics dashboard. Prefer a compact story from money spent to actual business result.
