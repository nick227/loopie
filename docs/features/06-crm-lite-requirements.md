# CRM-Lite Requirements

## Purpose
CRM-lite exists primarily to connect advertising activity to real business outcomes. It is not intended to become a full CRM in Phase 1.

## Minimum contact record
- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Company optional
- Lead/customer status
- Source
- Tags
- Created date

## Minimum lead data
- Contact
- Campaign
- Creative
- Deployment
- Platform
- Form submission timestamp
- Attribution identifiers

## Minimum sale data
- Contact
- Amount
- Date
- Optional note

## Basic lifecycle
Lead → Customer → Sale

## Core actions
- Add/import contact
- View source
- Change lead/customer status
- Mark as sale
- Enter revenue

## Audience foundation
Contacts should support simple reusable grouping by tags, status, source, campaign source, and date-based filters.

## Principle
The CRM should answer: **who came from this campaign, and did they buy?**
