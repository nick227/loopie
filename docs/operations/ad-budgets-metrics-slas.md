# Managed ad spending

The business pays Meta, Google, or similar. Midnight Creative runs the campaigns. Media hits **their** ad account. People who respond sit in LOOPIE. Our time to run the account is quoted separately from the $299 subscription.

Numbers below are **examples** (local US service, 2026). City, trade, and season will move them. They are for checking whether a budget can even learn — not a promised result.

---

## Dollars → views and clicks

**CPM** = cost per 1,000 times shown (Meta, most “discovery”).  
**CPC** = cost per click (Google Search; sometimes Meta).

```text
impressions ≈ (dollars ÷ CPM) × 1,000
clicks     ≈ impressions × CTR     or     dollars ÷ CPC
```

| | Cheap / quiet | Typical local | Competitive Search (HVAC, legal) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Meta CPM | $8 | $15 | $25–40 |
| Meta CTR | 1.5% | 1% | 0.5% |
| Google Search CPC | $8 | $20 | $40–80 |

At **$15 CPM, 1% CTR** (Meta):

| Spend | Views | Clicks |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $10 / day | ~670 | ~7 |
| $30 / day | ~2,000 | ~20 |
| $100 / day | ~6,700 | ~67 |
| $900 / month | ~60,000 | ~600 |
| $3,000 / month | ~200,000 | ~2,000 |

At **$20 CPC** (Search) — views barely matter; you pay for the click:

| Spend | Clicks |
| ---: | ---: |
| $10 / day | ~0.5 (often won’t deliver) |
| $30 / day | ~1.5 |
| $100 / day | ~5 |
| $900 / month | ~45 |
| $3,000 / month | ~150 |

Same $900 buys a lot of Meta views and a handful of Search clicks. Search is closer to “I need this now.” Neither number is a customer. A LOOPIE contact is a slice of clicks (often well under 20%, page-dependent). A booked job is smaller and later.

## Size and saturation

**Audience** = who the platform is allowed to show. A 15-mile radius might be 30k–80k adults. A metro trade, hundreds of thousands. A few ZIPs, a few thousand.

**Saturation** = showing the same people too often. Clicks drop, CPM often rises. You are paying again for people who already passed.

$30/day at $15 CPM ≈ 2,000 views/day ≈ 60,000/month. Against 40,000 people that is light. **$100/day** in that same pool is several views per person per week — mostly repeats. A town of 8,000 adults cannot eat $100/day on Meta. A metro Search campaign at $10/day often cannot win auctions at all. That is not “efficient”; it is **too little to run**.

Spend also has to match the shop: a one-truck crew cannot take 40 extra jobs next week. Names in LOOPIE with nobody on the phone are not a media success.

## Time

| Pace | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| $900 over 30 days (~$30/day) | Often enough on Meta to see if LOOPIE gets names, and for a quote to come back |
| $900 in 3 days | Spike, burn a small audience, no time to follow up |
| $900 in week 1, then off | Platform learns, then starves. Clock resets |

Run at least as long as that trade takes to go from click to booked job (often weeks). A three-day burst cannot show return. Raise spend only after contacts and follow-up are visible.

## Platforms

| | Buys | Weak when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meta / Instagram | Area + cheaper clicks | You need “near me” *today* |
| Google Search | Fewer, costlier, higher-intent clicks | Budget cannot buy enough clicks in a week |
| Maps / Local | People already on the map | No Business Profile |
| LOOPIE slots | Inventory we actually control | No place to put the ad |

$900 on **one** platform can teach. $900 split three ways ($10/day each) often teaches nothing. Second platform = second campaign in LOOPIE, after the first path creates contacts. Meta bids do not transfer to Search.

## Targeting we can run

Who is *allowed* to see the ad. Not the offer, not the page. Change one lever at a time.

**Place** — radius they will drive; towns/ZIPs they already serve; drop areas they will not. Default for local Meta. A 15-mile pond is tens of thousands; three ZIPs may be a few thousand (same $30/day saturates faster).

**Intent** — Search keywords (“emergency plumber”), negatives (DIY, jobs), Maps if they have a profile. Limit location so you are not buying another state. Metro plumber at $100/day: keywords and hours matter more than interest folders.

**Situation** — age, language, “homeowners.” A wider net: more views, fewer people who needed you today. Use when there is no list and the work is not emergency Search.

**LOOPIE lists** — past customers, quoted-not-won, quiet leads; exclude current customers. Highest leverage if the file is real and they may use it for ads. **~50 rows** often will not match. **Hundreds to thousands** usually will. Small lists saturate; pair the ad with LOOPIE email, do not replace it.

**Saw you already** — page visitors, ad engagers; exclude people who already booked. Needs traffic first. A $10/day campaign never fills this pool. Use after a cold campaign has been running, not as day-one targeting.

**Lookalike** — platform finds people like a customer (or form) list. Source quality in, garbage in. Needs **hundreds** of matched users, not a dozen. Still clip to the map they will serve.

**Hours / season** — ads when someone answers (overnight Search still costs $20). AC in June, boilers in October = new campaign, not a silent swap. Promo windows must last longer than click-to-book.

**LOOPIE-owned ads** — which page/site, plus session. No Meta interest graph. Destination still has to create a contact in the right account.

**Usual order:** place *or* Search (one platform) → hours that match the phone → exclude customers if the list is clean → after names exist, quoted-not-won and page visitors → lookalike only when the customer list is big enough.

## Use cases

Assume Meta $15 CPM / 1% CTR, Search $20 CPC, ~8% of clicks become a LOOPIE contact. Booking not estimated.

**HVAC, one town, $30/day Meta.** 15-mile radius. ~2,000 views, ~20 clicks/day; maybe ~50 contacts/month. Pond ~40k: room for 30 days. Search at this spend is ~1 click/day — skip it. After names exist, a little budget can go to quoted-not-won.

**Same shop, $100/day Meta, same town.** ~6,700 views/day, heavy repeats in 10–14 days. Widen only if they drive farther, or split: most still cold geo, some on visitors and past customers, different ads.

**Metro plumber, $100/day Search.** Emergency / “near me” terms, negatives, staffed hours. ~5 clicks/day, ~12 contacts/month. $30/day may not hold the auction. You saturate queries, not neighbors.

**Clinic, $50/day Meta.** Towns they serve, desk hours, language if needed. Retarget visitors after a month of traffic. Names may book 2–4 weeks out. Two weeks of spend cannot answer “did this pay.”

**Already at $5,000/month on four platforms, no LOOPIE names.** Not “add TikTok.” One campaign, one geo or keyword set, LOOPIE page. Rebuild customer/quoted lists after contacts exist.

**Our own LOOPIE ads, $30/day Meta.** Business owners in a geo, one page. Return is a paying account, later. Judge demos and Won in LOOPIE. Lookalike of Won only if that list is hundreds.

## Return

Return is **recorded sales you can tie to the campaign**, minus media. Until then you only have costs: spend, clicks, names, follow-up.

Report LOOPIE numbers (tracked / matched / platform / unknown). The tables say whether the budget is **in range to learn**. They do not say the ads will pay back.
