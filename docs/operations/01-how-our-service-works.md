# Internal Training: How Our Service Works

## What We Sell
We are not primarily selling CRM software. We are selling a managed customer-growth system supported by our software.

Our job is to help a business:
- reach the right people,
- capture and organize contacts,
- communicate consistently,
- follow up without dropping leads,
- move opportunities toward a sale,
- continue the relationship after the sale,
- measure what happened.

## The Core Loop
**Audience → Message → Response → Lead → Follow-Up → Sale → Future Outreach**

Our software makes this loop repeatable and visible.

## What the Client Experiences
The client should experience simplicity.

They should be able to answer:
- Who are we talking to?
- What are we sending?
- What needs a reply?
- What happened?
- What should we do next?

They should not need to understand our database structure, integrations, automation engine, or internal processes.

## What We Do Internally
Our team may:
- import and clean customer lists,
- create audiences,
- prepare messages,
- build reusable templates,
- configure follow-up,
- monitor replies,
- help organize leads,
- record or reconcile sales outcomes,
- prepare recommendations,
- report results.

Early clients may rely on us for nearly everything.

Long term, the software should allow businesses to handle these same workflows themselves.

## Our Operating Principle
**We do the work. The client stays in control.**

We prepare and recommend. The client approves important customer-facing decisions. Approved recurring processes can then run automatically.

## What Good Looks Like
A healthy account has:
- organized contact data,
- clear audience segments,
- approved messaging,
- reliable follow-up,
- visible lead status,
- no unanswered important activity,
- recorded outcomes,
- simple reporting.

## What We Avoid
- unnecessary complexity,
- jargon,
- sending without proper approval,
- duplicate customer records,
- manual work that can be safely standardized,
- vanity metrics without business context,
- overwhelming clients with software features.

## Team Mindset
Always think in terms of the customer relationship, not isolated tools.

Email, text, contacts, leads, sales, and automation are parts of one continuous process.
