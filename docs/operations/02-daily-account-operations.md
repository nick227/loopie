# Internal Training: Daily Account Operations

## Objective
Keep every client account organized, responsive, and moving forward.

## Start of Day
Review accounts for:
- new leads,
- unanswered replies,
- follow-ups due,
- failed sends,
- automation errors,
- pending approvals,
- overdue sales updates,
- unusual changes in results.

Work from exceptions first.

## New Lead Workflow
When a new lead appears:

1. Confirm the contact was created correctly.
2. Confirm source information is present when available.
3. Check whether the approved acknowledgment was sent.
4. Confirm the correct person is responsible for responding.
5. Watch for a reply.
6. Escalate if the lead is not handled within the client's agreed process.
7. Update the lead status as activity occurs.

## Message Preparation Workflow
Before a message is sent:

1. Confirm the purpose.
2. Confirm the audience.
3. Confirm channel eligibility.
4. Choose approved content or prepare a draft.
5. Review personalization.
6. Review links and calls to action.
7. Configure follow-up if needed.
8. Verify stop conditions.
9. Send for client approval when required.
10. Launch only after approval.

## Audience Checklist
Before sending, verify:
- the audience matches the intended group,
- duplicates are removed,
- opted-out recipients are excluded,
- required contact fields exist,
- audience count is reasonable,
- test contacts are not accidentally included.

## Follow-Up Workflow
Automation should be simple and explainable.

Typical pattern:
**Send → wait → check response → follow up → stop**

Always confirm:
- how long to wait,
- what counts as a response,
- when follow-up stops,
- what happens after a sale,
- what happens after an opt-out,
- when a human should take over.

## Sales Status
Use clear stages:
- New
- Contacted
- Qualified
- Quoted
- Won
- Lost

Do not change statuses merely to make reports look better.

Record real outcomes.

## Contact History
Important activity should be visible on the contact timeline:
- messages,
- replies,
- notes,
- status changes,
- quotes,
- sales,
- follow-up actions.

If information matters to the next person handling the customer, record it.

## End of Day
Before closing work:
- clear urgent unanswered activity,
- confirm failed sends were handled,
- document client decisions,
- verify scheduled sends,
- note unresolved issues,
- confirm tomorrow's critical follow-ups.

## Escalate Immediately
Escalate when:
- an integration is disconnected,
- messages are sending incorrectly,
- a large audience appears wrong,
- an automation is behaving unexpectedly,
- a client disputes a send,
- contact data appears corrupted,
- a high-value lead is being ignored.

## Internal Standard
The client should never need to discover an operational problem before we do.
