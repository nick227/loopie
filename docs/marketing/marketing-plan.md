# LOOPIE Go-To-Market Strategy & Execution Plan (Q3/Q4)

## Executive Summary
This document outlines our 90-day Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy for LOOPIE. The primary objective is to acquire ten qualified platform demos and secure two paying accounts at our $299/month tier. To validate our product-market fit and protect capital, this initial phase relies entirely on organic acquisition and direct sales outreach. Paid media is strictly prohibited until we hit these initial validation metrics.

The strategy requires a rigorous daily cadence. We will combine high-leverage field networking, high-volume digital outbound calling, and a focused digital presence to build a predictable pipeline. Above all, every prospect interaction must be logged in LOOPIE.

---

## 1. High-Leverage Field Networking

Field work is expensive and time-consuming. We do not use it for door-to-door sales of individual $299/month accounts. Instead, field sales is exclusively reserved for establishing high-leverage partnerships, securing affiliates, and accessing larger enterprise opportunities. 

### Strategic Referral Partnerships & Affiliates
*   **Target Profile:** Professionals who already serve our target market en masse, such as accounting firms, commercial insurance brokerages, regional web agencies, and local business associations.
*   **The Approach:** Take them to coffee or lunch. Understand their business model and demonstrate how recommending LOOPIE helps their clients stay organized (and generates mutual value).
*   **The Goal:** Cultivate an affiliate relationship. Before the meeting ends, ask for three warm introductions to business owners they know who are struggling to keep their sales pipeline organized.
*   **Operational Rule:** Add these introductions as new contacts in LOOPIE immediately after the meeting. Use the source tag `referral`. 

### Enterprise & High-Volume Access
*   **Target Profile:** Franchisors, regional trade groups, or large parent companies that dictate software choices for multiple local branches.
*   **The Approach:** Secure in-person meetings with operational directors or regional managers. Frame LOOPIE as the standardized solution for their entire network's lead management.
*   **The Goal:** Secure pilot programs that cover 5+ locations simultaneously.
*   **Operational Rule:** Track these larger entities in LOOPIE with the source tag `enterprise`.

---

## 2. Direct Outbound Acquisition

Regular $299/month accounts are acquired exclusively through high-volume phone and digital outbound. To generate consistent pipeline velocity, the sales team must execute this process daily to force meaningful conversations.

### The Daily Outbound Quota
*   **Volume Requirement:** Reach out to eight net-new prospects every business day.
*   **Execution Protocol:**
    1.  **Research:** Find eight qualified local businesses (plumbers, HVAC, roofers) and add them to LOOPIE.
    2.  **Email First:** Send our standard 'Email 1' directly through LOOPIE to ensure the message is tracked.
    3.  **Call:** Pick up the phone. If the owner answers, deliver our 20-second value proposition and ask to schedule a 20-minute demo.
    4.  **Voicemail:** If they don't answer, leave a brief 15-second voicemail outlining why you called, and log the attempt in LOOPIE.
    5.  **Social Follow-up:** Send a quick, polite direct message on Facebook or Instagram referencing your email and call, which often increases response rates.

### Capitalizing on Active Ad Spenders
*   **Target Profile:** Local businesses actively paying for Facebook Ads or Google Local Services.
*   **The Strategy:** These companies are already spending money to acquire leads, which means they feel the financial sting when a lead slips through the cracks due to poor follow-up.
*   **The Messaging:** Address this specific pain point directly. For example: *"I saw your ad on [Platform]. Are you tracking the leads you get from it in a centralized system, or are they getting lost in your inbox?"*
*   **Operational Rule:** Tag these prospects with the source `facebook-ads` or `google` to measure how well this segment converts.

## 3. Digital Positioning & Inbound Architecture

Our digital marketing strategy is designed to support the outbound sales team with a clear, unified message. We are optimizing for direct conversions (demos), not brand awareness.

### Unified Corporate Positioning
*   **Action:** Standardize all professional profiles across our digital channels (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram).
*   **The Core Message:** Ensure our value proposition is front and center: *"Keep every lead and follow-up in one place. Book a 20-minute walkthrough. $299/month."*
*   **Conversion Mechanism:** Every profile must include a direct link to the sales demonstration calendar.

### Content Distribution Cadence
*   **Volume Requirement:** Publish three targeted posts per week.
*   **Content Pillars:** Avoid generic industry fluff. Focus exclusively on these three themes:
    1.  **The Pain:** Discuss the financial risks and chaos of managing a sales pipeline using spreadsheets and personal email accounts.
    2.  **The Solution:** Highlight the operational advantage of writing and storing client emails directly within the CRM (LOOPIE's core feature).
    3.  **The Proof:** *(Once we secure two active accounts)* Share real testimonials detailing the specific time and administrative headache our clients have saved.
*   **Conversion Mechanism:** Every post must end with a call-to-action directing the prospect to book a demo.

### Inbound Lead Processing
*   **Action:** Monitor all social channels and inboxes daily for inquiries or comments.
*   **Operational Rule:** Anyone who interacts with our brand must be immediately added to LOOPIE as a contact. Use the source tag `social` or `website`. Respond promptly with a calendar link to drive them straight to a demo. Do not leave leads sitting in a social media inbox.

---

## 4. Operational Governance

Strict adherence to these operational rules is required. Our success relies on accurate data and disciplined daily execution.

1.  **Absolute System Reliance:** LOOPIE is our single source of truth. If a prospect or interaction is not recorded in LOOPIE, it didn't happen. External spreadsheets or offline notebooks are prohibited.
2.  **Financial Discipline:** We will maintain a zero-dollar budget for paid digital ads until we hit our primary validation milestone (10 completed demos and 2 paying customers generated organically).
3.  **Weekly Performance Review:** At the end of each week, we will review the data in LOOPIE. We will look at new contact acquisition, demo volume, and closed revenue, segmented by source. We will then double down on the channels that are actually producing results.
