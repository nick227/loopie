# LOOPIE First-Send Checklist

*Goal: Get the customer to send their first live email within 14 days of sign-up.*

## Phase 1: Setup
- [ ] **1. Account Ready:** Customer can successfully log in to LOOPIE.
- [ ] **2. List Received:** Customer provides their contact list (Excel/CSV format).
- [ ] **3. Import Done:** We clean the list and import it into LOOPIE.

## Phase 2: The First Send
- [ ] **4. Pick the Audience:** Together, choose one clear segment for the first email (e.g., 'past 6 months').
- [ ] **5. Draft & Approve:** We draft the first email. **The customer reviews and approves the exact words.** Do not send without explicit approval.
- [ ] **6. Push Send:** Send the approved email to a small batch (under 50 contacts) while the customer watches on screen. 

## Phase 3: Handoff
- [ ] **7. Transfer Control:** Book a 15-minute check-in for Day 7. The customer now runs LOOPIE for all future sends (unless a managed plan was explicitly purchased).
