# LOOPIE Online Marketing Campaign: Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

This is the daily execution manual. No high-level theory. These are the exact, step-by-step instructions required to run the online marketing engine. 

---

## 1. Daily Social Inbound Processing (Do this at 9:00 AM & 4:00 PM)
*Goal: Never let a lead sit in a social inbox.*

1.  **Open Inboxes:** Open Facebook Business Suite, Instagram Direct, and LinkedIn Messaging.
2.  **Scan for Intent:** Identify any comment or direct message asking a question, liking a product post, or requesting information.
3.  **CRM Entry:** 
    *   Open LOOPIE.
    *   Click "Add Contact". 
    *   Enter their Name and Social Handle/Link.
    *   Set Stage to `New`.
    *   Set Source to `social`.
4.  **The Reply:** Reply directly to them on the social platform using this exact script:
    *   *"Hey [Name], thanks for reaching out. The easiest way to see if LOOPIE works for you is a quick screen share. Do you have 20 minutes this week? Here is my calendar: [Link]"*
5.  **Log the Reply:** Copy your reply and paste it into a Note on their LOOPIE contact record.
6.  **Next Action:** Tag them in LOOPIE with `next:day3` to follow up if they don't book.

---

## 2. Weekly Content Execution Checklist

### Monday: The Pain Post
*Goal: Remind them that spreadsheets cost them money.*
1.  **Draft:** Write 3 short sentences outlining the problem of losing track of quotes.
2.  **Asset:** Take a screenshot of a messy, fake Excel spreadsheet.
3.  **Call to Action:** Add: *"Keep every lead and follow-up in one place. Book a walkthrough: [Link]"*
4.  **Publish:** Post to LinkedIn, Facebook Page, and Instagram at 11:00 AM local time.

### Wednesday: The Product Proof
*Goal: Show, don't tell.*
1.  **Record:** Open the Riverside Mechanical demo account in LOOPIE. Use Loom or QuickTime to record a 30-second screen capture. 
2.  **Action to Record:** Record yourself changing a lead status from "Quoted" to "Won" and logging a $299 sale.
3.  **Caption:** *"This takes 3 seconds in LOOPIE. Stop digging through your inbox to figure out who bought. Book a walkthrough: [Link]"*
4.  **Publish:** Post the video to all platforms at 1:00 PM local time.

### Friday: Social Proof
*Goal: Build trust through real results.*
1.  **Source:** Find a quote from a customer or (if none yet) share a specific metric of time you saved this week using LOOPIE.
2.  **Asset:** Use Canva or plain text to format the quote clearly.
3.  **Caption:** *"Real results from getting out of the inbox. See it for yourself: [Link]"*
4.  **Publish:** Post to all platforms at 10:00 AM local time.

## 3. The "Guerilla" Facebook Group Tactic (Do this Tuesday & Thursday)
*Goal: Generate organic inbound from high-density local business groups.*

1.  **Join Groups:** Ensure you are a member of 10 active local business groups (e.g., "Local Plumbers Network", "[City] Small Business Owners").
2.  **Search:** Use the group search bar. Type keywords: *"CRM"*, *"Software"*, *"Follow up"*, *"Leads"*, *"Lost track"*.
3.  **Engage (Do not pitch):** Find a post where an owner is asking for advice or complaining about organization.
4.  **The Script:** Reply with value first, then a soft mention:
    *   *"I used to lose quotes in my inbox all the time. Spreadsheets didn't work for me because I still had to switch tabs to email them. We actually ended up building a super simple tool just to fix this (keeps the email and the lead status on one screen). Happy to show you how we set it up if you're ever looking for a change."*
5.  **Follow Up:** If they reply or DM you, move immediately to **SOP 1 (Daily Social Inbound Processing)**.

---

## 4. Paid Ads Launch Checklist (Use ONLY after 10 organic demos)
*Goal: Launch the retargeting campaign flawlessly without wasting budget.*

1.  **Pixel Verification:** Ensure the Meta/Facebook Pixel is installed on the LOOPIE public page and firing correctly.
2.  **Audience Creation:** Go to Meta Ads Manager -> Audiences. Create a Custom Audience of "All Website Visitors in the last 30 days".
3.  **Campaign Setup:** 
    *   Objective: Sales / Conversions.
    *   Budget: Set Daily Budget to exactly $15.00.
4.  **Ad Creative Setup:**
    *   **Image:** Use a clean, simple screenshot of the LOOPIE 'Home' dashboard.
    *   **Primary Text:** *"Still managing your sales quotes in a spreadsheet? Keep every lead and follow-up in one place. See how LOOPIE works in 20 minutes."*
    *   **Headline:** *"Stop losing leads in your inbox."*
    *   **Destination URL:** Your Calendly booking link (MUST include UTM parameters: `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=retargeting`).
5.  **The Kill Switch Rule:** Check Ads Manager daily. If total Spend hits $150.00 and Calendly shows 0 booked demos from that UTM source, pause the campaign instantly. No exceptions.
