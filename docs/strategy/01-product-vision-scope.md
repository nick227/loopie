# Product Vision & Scope

## Product

A comprehensive **"Creative to Close"** marketing and sales operating system designed for businesses and agencies. The platform manages the entire lifecycle: generating creatives, deploying ad campaigns, capturing leads, nurturing them via consolidated messaging, and closing the deal with integrated quotes and billing.

## Core idea

The unified pipeline is the primary driver. An action taken at the top of the funnel (Creative/Ad) flows seamlessly down through Lead Generation, Omnichannel Nurturing, and ultimately converts into measurable Revenue (Billing/Close), with complete attribution tracking at every step.

## Primary user

A small or mid-sized business, agency, or internal sales and marketing team that wants one centralized surface for managing both their advertising spend and their sales pipeline, eliminating the need to jump between ad dashboards, CRMs, and billing platforms.

## Core loop

1. **Make or upload creative:** Centralized asset management.
2. **Deploy to platforms:** Push creatives to ad networks.
3. **Capture & enrich leads:** Convert anonymous traffic into qualified contacts via custom landing pages.
4. **Consolidated nurturing:** Communicate with leads via a unified omnichannel inbox (Email, SMS, Social).
5. **Close & bill:** Generate quotes and trigger invoices natively to complete the cycle.
6. **Track & iterate:** Analyze the complete pipeline (from ad spend to closed revenue) to improve the next batch.

## Phase 1 in scope

- Campaign creation and lifecycle management
- Asset library (Image, text, video, audio)
- Custom Landing Pages & Form Capture
- Platform deployments (Meta, Google, TikTok)
- Unified CRM-Lite contact and lead pipeline
- Consolidated Omnichannel Inbox
- Native Quoting & Billing Integrations (e.g., Stripe)
- Automated Drip Campaigns & Social Autoposting
- View → Click → Lead → Nurture → Sale full-funnel tracking

## Product principle

Keep the user focused on the complete pipeline: **what creative brought the lead in, how did we communicate with them, and how much revenue did they generate?**
