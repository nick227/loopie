# Product Vision & Scope

## Product
A campaign-first marketing management platform for businesses that create, deploy, compare, and improve advertising creative across multiple channels.

## Core idea
The campaign is the primary unit of work. A campaign combines a creative direction, assets, platforms, budget, dates, and measurable performance.

## Primary user
A small or mid-sized business, agency, or internal marketing team that wants one simple surface for managing creative and paid media without living inside multiple ad-platform dashboards.

## Core loop
1. Make or upload creative.
2. Create a campaign.
3. Deploy creative to ad platforms.
4. Track spend and outcomes.
5. Compare creative and platform effectiveness.
6. Use the results to improve the next creative.

## Phase 1 in scope
- Campaign creation and lifecycle management
- Asset library
- Image, text, image+text, video, and audio assets
- Platform deployments
- Budget and date tracking
- Performance rollups
- View → click → lead → sale funnel
- CRM-lite contact/lead/sale tracking
- Hosted/shareable creative URLs

## Explicitly secondary
- Full CRM
- Advanced marketing automation
- Deep email marketing
- Social scheduling
- Enterprise reporting
- Complex attribution modeling

## Product principle
Keep the user focused on one question: **what creative is running, where is it running, what did it cost, and what worked?**
