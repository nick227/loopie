# Product Vision

## Purpose
Build a simple communication and customer-management product for small and mid-sized businesses.

The product should help a business choose who to contact, send a message, automate reasonable follow-up, understand replies and customer history, move leads toward a sale, and see what happened.

## Core Promise
**Know who your customers are, send the right message, follow up automatically, and understand the result.**

## Product Position
The product should feel simpler than a traditional CRM and more customer-aware than a basic email-marketing tool.

**Mailchimp simplicity + lightweight CRM + sales follow-up + automation.**

## Primary User
A business owner, manager, salesperson, or marketer who needs to communicate with customers without learning complex CRM software.

## Phase 1 Mental Model
The primary surface is **Messages**.

Users should immediately understand:
1. Who should receive this?
2. What do I want to say?
3. How should I send it?
4. Should anything happen automatically afterward?
5. What happened after it was sent?

## Product Principles
- Familiar language over invented terminology.
- One obvious action at a time.
- Complex data model, simple UI.
- Automation should feel like assistance, not programming.
- Customer history should be visible in context.
- Sales progression should emerge naturally from communication.
- Major customer-facing actions remain under business control.
- Avoid exposing internal platform concepts unless users need them.

## Phase 1 Boundary
Included: contacts, audiences, email/text/social message composition, reusable content/templates, simple follow-up automation, replies/activity, lead/customer status, sales outcome tracking, and basic results.

Not included: ad management, lead scraping/discovery, advanced workflow builders, deep analytics, white-label management, agency/multi-account workflows, or advanced AI agents.

## Success
A first-time user should be able to import contacts, choose a group, send a message, configure one follow-up, and understand the resulting activity without training.
