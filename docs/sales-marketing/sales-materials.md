# LOOPIE Sales Materials and Assets

**Period:** 26 August 2026 – 24 November 2026  
**Owners:** Account Executive (uses daily), Marketing Coordinator (produces), Customer Success (onboarding kit), General Manager (approves claims and price), Software Engineer (demo account).

Do not build a brand book, a long slide deck, or ads until the kit below is in use. Every asset has one job: **book a 20-minute demo, close $299/month, or get a paying customer to send their first message.**

Claims we may make: contacts, messages, leads, and results in one login; email send that they can see; they stay in control of what goes out.  
Claims we may not make: live Meta/Google spend sync, SMS at scale, social auto-post to networks, “we will make you more revenue.”

## 2. Public page (Asset A)

One URL. No blog, no feature encyclopedia.

**Blocks, in order**

1. **Headline:** Keep every lead and follow-up in one place.  
2. **Subhead:** LOOPIE is for service businesses that already get inquiries from ads, referrals, or social and lose them across inbox, texts, and ad tools.  
3. **What you do in LOOPIE:** Import contacts. Send the next message. See who replied. Mark the job won or lost.  
4. **Price:** $299 per business per month. Cancel before the next bill. No setup fee on this offer.  
5. **What is not included:** We do not run your ads for you. Live ad-account sync and SMS are not part of this launch.  
6. **Button:** Book a 20-minute demo.  
7. Footer: terms, privacy, company legal name, support email.

Booking: Calendly (or equal), 20-minute slots, AE’s calendar, buffer 10 minutes. Confirmation email = Asset C, “demo confirmation.”

## 4. Outbound pack (Asset C)

**Who we write to:** owner or the person who answers the phone / runs ads for a local service business (trade, clinic, studio, shop).

**Personalize:** business name + where they seem to get work (Google, Facebook, Instagram, “people call the shop”). If you cannot see that in two minutes, skip the name or pick up the phone.

### Email 1 — first touch (day 0)

**Subject (pick one, do not rotate daily):**  
- Quick question about follow-up at {Company}  
- When a {Company} quote goes quiet  

**Body:**

> {First name} —  
> When someone inquires and then goes quiet, where does that thread live today: phone, personal inbox, Facebook, or a spreadsheet?  
> I can show you in 20 minutes how LOOPIE keeps that person, the follow-up, and whether they booked, on one screen. $299/month if it fits.  
> Here’s my calendar: {Booking URL}  
> {AE name}

### Email 2 — follow-up (day 3)

**Subject:** Re: {same thread}

> {First name} — bumping this once. If follow-up is already tight, say so and I’ll close the file. If it is not, the 20-minute slot is still open: {Booking URL}

### Email 3 — break-up (day 7)

> Last note from me. If you want the demo later, reply “later” and I’ll check next month. Otherwise I’ll assume it’s not a priority.

Stop after email 3 unless they reply.

### Instagram / Facebook DM (if that is how they actually market)

> Hi {Name} — I work on LOOPIE. We help shops that get leads from {ads/social} keep follow-up in one place instead of the inbox + the ad account. 20 minutes, no pitch deck. Want a time this week?

One follow-up three days later. Then stop.

### Voicemail (15 seconds)

> {First name}, {AE name} at LOOPIE. Calling because {Company} is getting inquiries and I want 20 minutes to show follow-up in one login. I’ll send a calendar link by email. {Phone}.

### Demo confirmation (auto)

> Confirmed: 20 minutes on {date} {time}.  
> Bring: how a new lead shows up for you, and (optional) a sample of names you’re allowed to show.  
> We’ll stay on one example, then price.  
> {Meet link}

### Demo no-show (same day)

> Missed you at {time}. I can hold {two options}. If neither works, reply with a day.

## 6. Close sheet (Asset E)

AE keeps this next to Stripe.

**Included in $299/month (this launch)**  
Login for the business. Contacts and audiences. Email messaging. Lead stages. Record a sale. See sends and replies we actually capture.

**Not included**  
We do not manage their ad accounts. Live platform spend/metrics sync is not sold. SMS and social publish to networks are not sold. Custom automations beyond one simple follow-up are not sold. We do not sit on their phone and close jobs.

**Commercial**  
Monthly. Cancel before the next bill. No setup fee. Refunds: GM only. No free plan. Do not offer $499 or annual unless GM opened that test in writing.

**To start they give**  
A person who will log in. A contact export. Confirmation they may email those people.

## 8. Onboarding kit (Asset G)

**CSV columns (one row per person):**  
`name, email, phone, company, source, tags, last_job_or_inquiry_date`

CS email after Closed won (same day):

> You’re in. Reply with (1) who will log in, (2) this CSV filled or an export from your current tool, (3) confirmation we may email these people.  
> This week we import, you approve the first follow-up, we send to a small group—not the whole list.  
> {Login URL}

**First-send checklist (CS, 7 steps)**

1. User can log in.  
2. List imported; duplicates and obvious opt-outs pulled.  
3. Audience: quoted-and-quiet or past-6-months — pick one.  
4. They approve the exact email text.  
5. Send to ≤50.  
6. Show them the send in LOOPIE.  
7. Book a 15-minute check on day 7.

Live = step 6 done.
